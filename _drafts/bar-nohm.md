Small plates for sharing. Overall the food quality was very good, but Korean food isn't my favorite. I would recommend making a reservation. The skewers were really good, especially the wagyu. The duck was fantastic.

## Chicken Wings

Pretty good. Slathered in Korean BBQ sauce and cotija.

## Wagyu Skewer

Very good.

## Chicken Heart Skewer

Good, but could have been crispier and saucier imo.

## Chicken Tender Skewer

Good, very tender.

## Enoki Wrapped in Pork Skewer

Good, could have used a little more sauce as well.

## Bluefish Sashimi

Pretty good.

## Kimchi Rice

Kimchi fried rice. Basically like a Korean jambalaya. It was decent, but I don't love kimchi.

## Ghost Daq

Tasted very heavily of liquor, which I didn't like.
