## Bruschetta

Excellent.

## Butternut Squash Lasagna

A little sweet, very cheesy and pretty good.

## Sicilian

A little on the spicy side but the meats were very good. The sauce and meat to noodle ratio was high.

## Gelato and Tiramisu

Tiramisu was sharing size and very good. The lady fingers were very spongy and the cream was just right.
