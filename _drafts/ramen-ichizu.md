Heard a lot of good things about this place. They recently opened so some of the problems we ran into may just be growing pains and them getting things figured out. It took over 30 minutes for us to get our food after we ordered. They completely forgot about the karaage and we had to ask about it. The food itself was pretty good though.

## Karaage

Very good. Dark meat and flaky breading. Well seasoned.

## Tokusei Shoyu (All Toppings)

The noodles were good. Very Q. I'm more of a fan of tonkotsu ramen, but as far as shoyu ramen goes, this is pretty good. The broth had a good flavor.

## Tan Tan Ramen

Same noodles, i.e., good. Broth had a little 麻辣 (mala) spiciness to it.
