Vibe and decor is very authentic Chinese. Most of the customers that were there were Chinese.

## Stir Fry Cabbage

Texture and flavor were both very good.

## Fuqi Feipian 夫妻肺片

![Fuqi feipian 夫妻肺片](/assets/img/posts/zhu-ting-ji/fuqi-feipian.jpg)

Beef shank, beef giblets, celery, cilantro. $18.99.

Flavor was good, just a little too spicy for me.

## Xiao Long Bao 小笼包

![Xiao long bao 小笼包](/assets/img/posts/zhu-ting-ji/xiao-long-bao.jpg)

Six per order, pork or chicken, with chicken broth. $12.99.

Flavor is good (improved from when they first opened). It was cool that you can see them make it through the glass like Din Tai Fung.

## Sichuan Spicy Boiled Fish 水煮鱼

![Sichuan spicy boiled fish 水煮鱼](/assets/img/posts/zhu-ting-ji/sichuan-spicy-boiled-fish.jpg)

Fish, lettuce, bean sprouts, chili, Szechuan pepper. $22.99.

## Fried Crispy Pork 小酥肉

![Fried crispy pork 小酥肉](/assets/img/posts/zhu-ting-ji/fried-crispy-pork.jpg)

Pork belly, starch, flour, served with a spiced dry chili dip. $16.99.

## Mapo Tofu 麻婆豆腐

![Mapo tofu 麻婆豆腐](/assets/img/posts/zhu-ting-ji/mapo-tofu.jpg)

Ground pork, tofu, Szechuan pepper. $13.99.
