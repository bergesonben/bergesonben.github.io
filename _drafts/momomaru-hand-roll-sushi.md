The nori was crunchy. The flavor and the texture of the rice was very good.

However, the fish all did not have much flavor, with the exception of the spicy tuna, but even then it just tasted spicy.

Heard really good reviews from others so might be worth retrying. From the flavor of the fish alone I think I much prefer HandoSake.
