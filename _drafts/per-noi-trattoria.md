Not too expensive.

## Bruschetta

Good. Pesto on baguettes with mozzarella, cherry tomatoes, and balsamic glaze.

## Fettuccine Alfredo

Very good. This might be the best fettuccine alfredo I've ever had.

## Chicken Special

Unsure what it would be called, because it was an off-menu special. It was chicken covered in mozzarella(?) and sauce - very good.

## "Filet Mignon"

Didn't seem like filet mignon at all, but it was good. The peppercorn sauce was very good.
