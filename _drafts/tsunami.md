Pretty good sushi on a good day. I used to go to the one in Lehi on Sunday afternoons and sat at the bar to have the chef do omakase. That was fantastic, but at other Tsunami locations and ostensibly other chefs, it's hit or miss. They have some pretty good rolls though: P.D., Something Tropical, and Jenny.
