They have rave reviews and I've gotten several very favorable recommendations for it. However, I wasn't that impressed.

## Garlic Naan

Thin and not that flavorful.

## Lamb Curry

Very heavy on the tomato. It tasted like a red sauce that you would put on pasta. The lamb itself was good.

## Shrimp Coconut Kurma

Overly sweet.

We got them both with mild spiciness; I wonder if getting them with medium spice would have helped.
