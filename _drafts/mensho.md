Parking could be a problem. Very pricey.

## Chicken Karaage

![Chicken karaage with sauce](/assets/img/posts/mensho/chicken-karaage-with-sauce.jpg)

$15. 

It was good, but not great. Housemade sauce poured on it with various garnishes. I don't love that, I'd rather just a simple karaage with no sauce and just seasoning.

## Signature Toripaitan

![Signature toripaitan](/assets/img/posts/mensho/signature-toripaitan.jpg)

$29. 

Very interesting. The broth was really thick, flavorful and rich. The two eggs were well seasoned. I don't usually like the runny boiled eggs in ramen, but I liked these.

Four kinds of meat! Duck with truffle sauce, chicken, pork, and wagyu. The wagyu was tender and smoky - really good.

The noodles were decent, not my favorite.
