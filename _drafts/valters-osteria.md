Overall the food was good, but just doesn't feel like it's worth it for the high price tag.

## Bruschetta

Free appetizer. Probably the only bruschetta that I've liked.

## Avocado in Bianco con Granchio

Half avocado, arugula, fresh crab, baby shrimp, and four cheeses.

Flavor was on the bland side, but still pretty good. Feels way overpriced at $38.

## Ravioli 4 Formaggi

Fresh homemade ravioli in a four cheese cream sauce with asparagus.

Best ravioli I've ever had. Texture of the ravioli is perfect.

## Nana in Porchetta Savinese

A typical Valter's family Sunday dinner recipe: fennel crusted duck breast in their cognac and grape sauce.

Very tasty, but also feels overpriced at $52.
