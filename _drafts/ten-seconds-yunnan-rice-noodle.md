The rice noodles were very [Q](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q_texture). Broth was decent. The meats, veggies, etc that go in the soup were just ok. The biggest draw here is that the noodles are served in stone bowl that keeps the noodle warm for the whole meal.
