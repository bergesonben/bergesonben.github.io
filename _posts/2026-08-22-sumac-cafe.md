## Tahdeeg

Liked the flavor. The rice was a little crunchier than I would have liked. It tasted really good a day later after they softened a bit.

## Dolmas

First time having dolmas and I loved it. It has a bit of sweetness to it that I'm not sure if it's from the grape leaves.

## Bone-in Lamb

So tender and flavorful. The rice that came with it was very good.
