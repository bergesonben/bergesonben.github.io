## Option A - $38.00

The "A - Standard" all-you-can-eat tier: nigiri, rolls, hand rolls, poke, sides, and tabletop BBQ, including wagyu otoro, wagyu brisket, beef tongue, ribeye or NY steak, and pork belly.

### Sushi

Wasn't bad, but nothing special. I mostly got it so I'd have something to eat while I was waiting for the meat to arrive and the grill to heat up.

### Wagyu Otoro

No matter how I cooked it, it always crumbled apart. I don't know how much I lost between the grates.

### Wagyu Brisket Point

Limited to one order per person. Very good.

### Wagyu Brisket

My favorite. Perfect wagyu texture, seasoned with just a bit of sesame oil and salt.

### Wagyu Yaki Shabu

Tied for second place with the Wagyu Brisket Point.

### Fried Gyoza

Decent.

### Chicken Karaage

Decent for the most part, though one piece had entirely too much breading.

## Conclusion

At $38.00, it's one hell of a deal to eat wagyu brisket to your heart's content.
