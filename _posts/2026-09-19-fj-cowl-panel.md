On a recent trip down to Cedar City for the Utah Shakespeare Festival, we encountered a torrential downpour, which caused the driver side cowl on my FJ to fly off completely. It was already loose, a problem that I've known about for the last year or so, but deemed unnecessary to fix. It was being held on by almost-20-year-old brittle plastic clips. Had I known how much time and money it would take to replace it with a new part, I might have turned around on the freeway to go look for it. Maybe not though, as I have a deep-seated loathing of getting soaked when I didn't plan for it.

## Prep Work
![Respirator selfie](/assets/img/posts/fj-cowl/respirator.jpg)
I was warned several times by different sources that the 2K clear is very bad for your lungs.

- Test fit the panel with the clips; lose the clips down the abyss between the fender and the wheel well liner🤦; buy another set off Amazon
- Set up a fan in the garage to make sure airflow is one direction, taking the overspray away
- Scratch the primered panel evenly with the red Scotch-Brite Pads to promote adhesion. Done outside to keep dust out of the garage
- Hang the panel from the rafters so I can get a good angle to spray all around
- Wipe down with wax & grease remover - two passes
- Use tack cloth to gently wipe the panel to remove any other contaminants
- One light coat of adhesion promoter

## Spraying
- Three base coats, with roughly 10-15 minutes between each
- 30 minutes after last coat, apply clear coat. Two clear coats with 15 minutes between each

## Install
This was by far the worst step. I broke another clip and lost another clip attempting to get the panel on straight. After fighting with it for over an hour, I finally gave up and called it good enough. I suspect I need to bend the plastic pieces holding the cowl at the center of the windshield to get everything lined up, but I'm unsure how to do that without breaking anything.

## How it turned out
Overall, I'm quite happy with how it turned out as a first time job. The paint matches pretty well. There's a little bit of orange peel going on. But at a glance, I don't think it's obvious that it isn't stock.
