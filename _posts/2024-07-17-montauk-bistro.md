Small hole in the wall restaurant. Everything is made in-house.

## Potstickers

The contents are good, but the potstickers were more like dumplings.

## Banh Mi

Really good. The bread is not too hard and the ratio of vegetables and meat and sauce was just right.

## Thai Tea Chai

Not too sweet. Delicious.
