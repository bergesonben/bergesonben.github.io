## Cuban Sampler Platter

![Golden-fried yuca, double-fried tostones, hand-rolled ham croquetas, and sweet caramelized maduros](/assets/img/posts/la-cubana/cuban-platter.jpg)

Golden-fried yuca, double-fried tostones, hand-rolled ham croquetas, and sweet caramelized maduros. 

## OG Miami Cuban (Sandwich)

![Cuban sandwich in a takeout container](/assets/img/posts/la-cubana/cuban-sandwich.jpg)

Slow roasted Cuban style pork, layered with ham, Swiss cheese, mustard and pickles with a little love of butter pressed till crispy. I'm not a big pickle guy, but this was a good combination of flavors. 

## Conclusion

Would get again.
