By and large I haven't had very good experiences at restaurants on the strip in Las Vegas. Usually they're overpriced and overhyped. This, however, was a pleasant surprise. Booking with the "Make a Night of It" offer through Hilton included a $75 dining credit available at limited restaurants, which included the Famous Foods food court. Geylang Claypot Rice is one of the stalls. 

## Chicken Claypot Rice

![Chicken claypot rice with Chinese sausage, greens, and scallions](/assets/img/posts/geylang-claypot-rice/chicken-claypot-rice.jpg)

The dish had a nice umami and spiciness to it. It included a generous portion of both types of meats, the chicken and the chinese sausage. 

## Conclusion

I wouldn't go out of my way to go there, but if you happen to be in Resort World and need a quick lunch, it's a good choice.
