## Braised Pork Belly

![Braised pork belly](/assets/img/posts/tiburon/braised-pork-belly.jpg)

"Chef's Favorite." Five hour "dry" braised Duroc pork belly, triple cream Brie, and caramelized balsamic onions. $18.

## Bread and Sorbet

Every entrée comes with a salad, fresh bread with house butter, and a homemade palate-cleansing sorbet.

![Bread with house butter](/assets/img/posts/tiburon/bread-and-butter.jpg)

![Palate-cleansing sorbet](/assets/img/posts/tiburon/sorbet.jpg)

## Ahi Tuna

![Ahi tuna](/assets/img/posts/tiburon/ahi-tuna.jpg)

Seven-pepper and black sesame crusted tuna, seared rare, topped with soy-honey marinated shredded carrot, served with pineapple fried rice. $43.

The fish was good, but the fried rice was mushy.

## New Zealand Elk

![New Zealand elk](/assets/img/posts/tiburon/new-zealand-elk.jpg)

House specialty. Char-grilled elk tenderloin with creamy mushroom duxelles and a green peppercorn demi-glace. $65.

## White Chocolate Cheesecake

![White chocolate cheesecake](/assets/img/posts/tiburon/white-chocolate-cheesecake.jpg)

Salted pretzel crust, vanilla bean crème anglaise, and raspberry compote. $12.
